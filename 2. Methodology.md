\# Methodology

\## Geospatial Disease Surveillance & Intervention Prioritization

\---

\### 1. Study Area Definition

Administrative boundaries for Kisumu, Siaya, Busia, Kakamega, Vihiga and Bungoma counties were extracted from geoBoundaries. Both ward-level and county-level layers were created and reprojected to \*\*EPSG:32636\*\* (WGS 84 / UTM Zone 36N).

\---

\### 2. Data Preparation

| Component | Source | Processing Summary |

|------------------------|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|

| Malaria Risk | Malaria Atlas Project (PfPR 2024) | Clipped, reprojected, standardized to 1–5 score |

| Population | WorldPop 2025 (100 m constrained) | Clipped and reprojected |

| Child Population | WorldPop Age/Sex Structure 2026 | Ages 0–9 aggregated and converted to vulnerability score |

| Rainfall | CHIRPS (March–May mean, 2021–2026) | Seasonal total calculated and scored 1–5 |

| Surface Water | JRC Global Surface Water Occurrence | Tiles merged, clipped, and scored 1–5 |

| Health Facilities | Kenya Master Health Facility Registry | Filtered, cleaned, and used to create density surface |

\---

\### 3. Indicator Standardization

All continuous indicators were standardized to a common \*\*1–5 scale\*\*:

\- \*\*1\*\* = Very Low

\- \*\*2\*\* = Low

\- \*\*3\*\* = Moderate

\- \*\*4\*\* = High

\- \*\*5\*\* = Very High

\---

\### 4. Health System Pressure

Facility density was calculated using Kernel Density Estimation with a 10 km search radius. Population pressure was then derived using the following formula:

Population Pressure = Population / (Facility Density + 0.001)

The resulting pressure surface was classified into a 1–5 score using percentile breaks.

\---

\### 5. Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)

The final \*\*Disease Surveillance Priority Index (DSPI)\*\* was calculated using the following weighted combination:

DSPI = (0.40 × Malaria Risk) + (0.25 × Environmental Suitability) + (0.20 × Population Vulnerability) + (0.15 × Health System Pressure)

\*\*Weights applied:\*\*

| Component | Weight |

|-------------------------------|--------|

| Malaria / Disease Risk | 40% |

| Environmental Suitability | 25% |

| Population Vulnerability | 20% |

| Health System Pressure | 15% |

\---

\### 6. Priority Classification

The continuous DSPI was classified into five relative priority classes (Very Low to Very High) using quintiles for mapping and reporting purposes.

\---

\### 7. Limitations

\- Environmental suitability is a spatial proxy and does not equal actual disease transmission.

\- Population datasets are modelled estimates.

\- Facility density reflects geographic distribution only and does not capture service quality or capacity.

\- MCDA results depend on the selected weights.

\- Limited malaria data coverage in some highland border areas required gap-filling or exclusion of a small number of cells.
