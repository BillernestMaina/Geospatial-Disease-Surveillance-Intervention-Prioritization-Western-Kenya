\# Data Sources

\## Geospatial Disease Surveillance & Intervention Prioritization

\---

\### 1. Malaria Risk

| Item | Details |

|------|---------|

| \*\*Dataset\*\* | Malaria Atlas Project – \*Plasmodium falciparum\* Parasite Rate |

| \*\*Product used\*\* | Global Pf Parasite Rate (Kenya extract, 2024) |

| \*\*Source\*\* | \[Malaria Atlas Project Data Platform\](https://data.malariaatlas.org/) |

| \*\*Role in project\*\* | Primary disease risk layer |

| \*\*Notes\*\* | Modelled surface. Standardized to a 1–5 risk score. |

\---

\### 2. Population

| Item | Details |

|------|---------|

| \*\*Dataset\*\* | WorldPop Population Counts |

| \*\*Product used\*\* | Kenya 2025 Constrained population count (100 m) |

| \*\*Source\*\* | \[WorldPop / HDX\](https://hub.worldpop.org/) |

| \*\*Role in project\*\* | Total population exposure and denominator for pressure calculations |

| \*\*Notes\*\* | Constrained (settlement-masked) product used. |

\---

\### 3. Age / Sex Structure (Vulnerability)

| Item | Details |

|------|---------|

| \*\*Dataset\*\* | WorldPop Age and Sex Structure |

| \*\*Product used\*\* | Kenya 2026 Age/Sex structures (1 km, UN-adjusted) |

| \*\*Source\*\* | \[WorldPop\](https://hub.worldpop.org/) |

| \*\*Role in project\*\* | Estimation of child population (ages 0–9) as a vulnerability proxy |

| \*\*Notes\*\* | Ages under 1, 1–4, and 5–9 were aggregated. |

\---

\### 4. Rainfall

| Item | Details |

|------|---------|

| \*\*Dataset\*\* | CHIRPS (Climate Hazards Group InfraRed Precipitation with Station data) |

| \*\*Product used\*\* | Monthly rainfall, March–May mean (2021–2026) |

| \*\*Source\*\* | \[CHC CHIRPS Data Repository\](https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/) |

| \*\*Role in project\*\* | Environmental suitability – seasonal rainfall component |

| \*\*Notes\*\* | Long rains (March–May) selected as the primary seasonal window. |

\---

\### 5. Surface Water

| Item | Details |

|------|---------|

| \*\*Dataset\*\* | JRC Global Surface Water |

| \*\*Product used\*\* | Water Occurrence (1984–2024) |

| \*\*Source\*\* | \[Global Surface Water Explorer\](https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/) |

| \*\*Role in project\*\* | Environmental suitability – persistent / frequent surface water |

| \*\*Notes\*\* | Two tiles (30E\_10N and 30E\_0N) were mosaicked to cover the study area. |

\---

\### 6. Health Facilities

| Item | Details |

|------|---------|

| trier \*\*Dataset\*\* | Kenya Master Health Facility Registry (KMHFR) |

| \*\*Product used\*\* | National facility list with coordinates |

| \*\*Source\*\* | \[Kenya Master Health Facility Registry\](https://kmhfr.health.go.ke/) |

| \*\*Role in project\*\* | Health system footprint and facility density |

| \*\*Notes\*\* | Filtered to the six study counties, operational facilities only, and records with valid coordinates. |

\---

\### 7. Administrative Boundaries

| Item | Details |

|------|---------|

| \*\*Dataset\*\* | geoBoundaries |

| \*\*Product used\*\* | Kenya ADM1 (Counties) and finer administrative units (Wards) |

| \*\*Source\*\* | \[geoBoundaries\](https://www.geoboundaries.org/) |

| \*\*Role in project\*\* | Study area definition, clipping, and zonal statistics |

| \*\*Notes\*\* | Used to create both county-level and ward-level analysis units. |

\---
